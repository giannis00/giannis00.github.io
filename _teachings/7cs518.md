## Overview

This postgraduate module covers the core methods of natural language processing, from statistical language models and n-gram approaches to neural architectures designed for sequential data. Students examine the theoretical foundations and practical implementation of models for text understanding and generation.

## Topics

- Statistical language models and n-grams
- Text preprocessing and feature extraction
- Recurrent Neural Networks (RNNs)
- Long Short-Term Memory networks (LSTMs)
- Sequence-to-sequence modelling
- Introduction to transformer-based language models

## Companion notebooks

The open course [From Words to Meaning: Natural Language Processing in Python](/teaching/#nlp) covers the topics of this module in 18 hands-on notebooks that run in Google Colab, and goes further into modern language models. It is a general course, not tied to this module, but the table shows where to look each week.

| Unit | Topic | Notebooks in *From Words to Meaning* |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Introduction to NLP | 1 |
| 2 | Basic text processing in Python | 1, 2, 3 |
| 4 | Language modelling, part-of-speech tagging, named entities | 6, 7 |
| 5 | Text representation | 8, 10 |
| 6 | Formal languages and automata | 2, 4, 5 |
| 7 | Recurrent networks and LSTMs | 12 |
| 8 | Sequence-to-sequence learning | 13 |
| 9 | Convolutional networks for text | 14 |
| 10 | Text from the web | 16 |
| 12 | Summarisation | 17 |

Text classification is covered in notebook 9. Notebooks 11, 15 and 18 go beyond the module: topic modelling, subword tokenisation and decoding, and retrieval-augmented generation.

The machine learning and neural network foundations of units 2, 3 and 11 are covered in [From Python to Deep Learning and Fine-Tuning](/teaching/#deep-learning), notebooks 8 to 13.
